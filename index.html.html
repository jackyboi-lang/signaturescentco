Orders

All fragrance decants are poured from authenticated retail bottles. Prices are listed in AUD and include GST where applicable. We ship within Australia only.

Returns

If you're not satisfied with an order, contact us within 14 days of delivery. Because decants are opened and poured to order, we assess return and refund requests case by case rather than offering automatic returns.

Age restriction

Some fragrances contain ingredients not suitable for very young children. Our products are intended for adult use.

Liability

Fragrance is a personal, subjective product — sillage, longevity, and skin chemistry vary from person to person, and we can't guarantee a specific experience.

Changes

We may update these terms from time to time; continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated terms.
